Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026 · applies to spidercrawler.site

AfterKey holds what you choose to write, and as little else as possible. This page says exactly what that is.

What is collected

  • Your email address, when you create an account or join the updates list.
  • Technical details of that submission: the time, the IP address the request came from, and the browser's user-agent string. These exist to stop automated abuse of the form and to keep an accurate record of consent.

Nothing else. There are no cookies, no analytics scripts, no advertising tags, and no third-party fonts or libraries. Every file needed to display this page is served from this domain.

Why it is held

To run your account, to send the messages you ask for, and to prevent the forms being used to send unsolicited mail. That is the whole purpose.

Who sees it

Email delivery is handled by Resend, which processes the address solely to send the message. Addresses are not sold, rented, or shared for marketing by anyone else. The list is stored in a database on this server, not in a third-party CRM.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists. Ask and it is deleted, together with the entries inside it and the delivery log. No reason is required.

Removal

Reply to any message from AfterKey, or write to afterkey01@gmail.com, and the address is deleted. No reason is required.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date above changes with it. Vault contents are never read by us and never used for anything other than holding them for you.

Account and legacy records

AfterKey stores your private vault files, account details, nominated loved ones, check-in history, and verification decisions on this server. Legacy invitations do not expose vault content. Access requires approved activation, email verification, identity review, and an independent recipient account.

Closing an account disables access immediately. Records and files remain in private storage and backups for recovery and retention; closure is not permanent erasure. Contact us to discuss retention or a deletion request. Verification references are available only to authorized reviewers.
