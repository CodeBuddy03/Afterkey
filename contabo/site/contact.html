Questions about your account, a request to delete your data, or a situation the vault does not yet handle: send it in writing and you will get a reply from a human.

afterkey01@gmail.com

Mail from AfterKey always arrives from no-reply@spidercrawler.site and always points back to this domain. If a message from AfterKey asks you for a password, a code, or a payment, it did not come from us.

What to include

  • The address you signed up with, so the account can be found.
  • What you would like changed, removed, or explained.
  • Any message reference, if your question is about a specific email.

Deletion

Say the word and the account is removed, together with the entries inside it and the delivery log. No reason is required, and no proof of identity beyond writing from the registered address.

Response time

Usually within two working days. If it takes longer, it is because the message needed a careful answer.
