The workflow

Three decisions, then it is kept.

AfterKey is built around a short, deliberate sequence. Nothing is shared until you have named the person and the moment.

01

Gather what only you know

Where the accounts live. Which advisor to call. The letter you have been meaning to write. One vault instead of a drawer of loose instructions.

02

Name the person it belongs to

A partner, a sibling, an executor, a friend. Each entry is addressed to someone specific, not posted somewhere and left to be found.

03

Keep it sealed

The entry is sealed to your account with the recipient and the moment recorded beside it. Nothing is published, indexed or handed anywhere else.

Entries stay sealed to your account. After an independently approved legacy activation, the person you nominated receives an invitation to begin verification.

The handoff

What the person you name receives.

One thoughtful invitation from AfterKey. A simple, secure path through email verification, identity review, and an account of their own.

01

An email, not the contents

02

Verification, at their own pace

They verify a six-digit code sent to their own email, complete an independent identity and inheritance review, and create secure credentials of their own.

Privacy and security

Built to hold things you would not put in a chat app.

Privacy is the product. These are the protections running on this site today, written plainly so you can hold us to them.

Entries stay private

What you write is stored on this server and readable only through your signed-in session. No other account can open it, and nothing is published or indexed.

Encrypted in transit

Every page and every request travels over HTTPS with HSTS. A strict content policy allows resources from this domain only.

Passwords are never stored in readable form

Each password is salted and hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 at 600,000 rounds. Sessions are random tokens, kept only as hashes, in a cookie that page scripts cannot read.

No trackers, no third-party scripts

No analytics, no advertising pixels, no external CDN. Typefaces and the 3D engine are served from this domain, so reading this page tells no one else that you did.

The handoff stays in your hands

Invitations never authorize access by themselves. Private content remains protected until activation, email, identity, account, and recovery checks are complete. A missed Life Check never proves death.

Questions

The short answers.

Is AfterKey live?

Yes. You can register, maintain your vault, manage loved ones and Life Checks, and secure your account. Legacy access requires an approved activation and a verified claim through the recipient’s own account. The original owner’s password or OTP is never needed.

What happens to what I prepare?

An entry stays sealed to your account. You name the recipient and the moment, and the entry is stored for you alone. Nothing is published, searchable or shared with anyone else.

Do you sell or share email addresses?

No. An address is used for AfterKey only, stored on this server, and removed on request.

Can I use it for someone else?

The vault is designed for the person preparing it. If you are helping a parent or partner set theirs up, write to us. We would like to understand that case properly before building for it.
